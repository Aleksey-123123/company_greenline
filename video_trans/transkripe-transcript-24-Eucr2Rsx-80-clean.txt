Bitrix24 is not only a CRM but also a system for managing production and work performed by employees without Bitrix24 access. This is accomplished using the Production and Work Management application. It allows you to connect your performers, production teams, and contractors who work outside the office and are not Bitrix24 users to CRM transactions. These could be temporary employees or staff who simply don't need access to the corporate CRM. Let's look at a case study of a construction company that builds small prefabricated houses for private clients. In the turnkey house construction contract, we've reached the final stage: construction on the client's site. This isn't a sale, but the final pipeline that closes the main process: delivery of materials to the client's site and assembly of the turnkey house as per

the contract. The transaction has stages, and a responsible manager is assigned in the office. The work itself is performed by mobile contractors who arrive on-site according to a work order. Their task is not only to build the house but also to deliver the work to the client. Ideally, the client's manager should be informed about every stage of the work without leaving the office. With the Production and Work Management app, company employees, working in Bitrix24, monitor all stages of work on-site. First, you need to add contractors through the app and grant them access. In the settings, you create accounts for these workers, and they don't necessarily have to be full-time employees. For example, Sergey Ivanov is the foreman of a seasonal crew. He doesn't have access to Btrix24 and isn't a user of the
